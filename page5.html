You Probably Don't Realize…

There are little things you did that made a difference. They may have seemed ordinary to you, but they meant more to me than you probably realized. I noticed them, I appreciated them, and I still remember them. Like talking to me late at night, telling me about your day, your family, your friends, yourself, and even the little things about your mood. Doing things I asked for, checking up on me, and simply taking care of me in your own way. Tolerating my nonsense, asking me to do little things for you, and even reacting to me when I managed to irritate you. Looking back, even those small moments mean something to me. They made me feel that I mattered, and I’m genuinely grateful for them.
